Lessons

  1. 1 Draw a Use-Case Diagram Actors, boundary, associations, «include» and «extend» — built in six moves, with a quiz and a paper task.

  4. 4 Draw a Sequence Diagram Lifelines, activations, DAO participants, alt frames — and where every rule check belongs.
  5. 5 Draw a Collaboration Diagram Convert the sequence diagram: object links, nested message numbers, guards, and iteration.
  6. 6 Draw a VOPC Diagram Harvest every message into an operation, then run the four-artifact consistency sweep.
  7. 7 Design a Subsystem Façade, service, DAO, repository — and the three principles with their consequences.
  8. 8 Timed Mock — Full Part A A fresh clinic scenario, seven tasks, 40 marks, a built-in timer, and per-task model answers.
  9. 9 The Real Practice Paper The official sample exam, timed: equipment reservation, design-level layers, and mark-calibrated model answers.
