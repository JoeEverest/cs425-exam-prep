# CS425 UML Diagram Resources

## Knowledge

- [CS425 Examination Preparation Guide (professor's handout, supplied by the user)](./MISSION.md)
  The authoritative source for scope and grading language. Use for: which
  diagrams appear, the consistency check, and the exact terms the professor
  uses (boundary/control/entity, DAO, façade, VOPC).
- [OMG UML 2.5.1 Specification](https://www.omg.org/spec/UML/2.5.1/About-UML)
  The primary source for notation. Use for: exact arrow styles, «include» and
  «extend» direction, lifeline and activation notation.
- [uml-diagrams.org — Use Case Diagrams](https://www.uml-diagrams.org/use-case-diagrams.html)
  Clear notation reference with examples. Use for: quick checks on use-case
  notation and actor rules.
- [uml-diagrams.org — Sequence Diagrams](https://www.uml-diagrams.org/sequence-diagrams.html)
  Use for: lifelines, activations, message types, combined fragments.
- [Book: _UML Distilled_ (3rd ed.) — Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/books/uml.html)
  Short, trusted overview of practical UML. Use for: what matters versus what
  is ceremony; sequence vs. communication diagram trade-offs.
- [Book: _Applying UML and Patterns_ (3rd ed.) — Craig Larman](https://www.craiglarman.com/wiki/index.php?title=Book_Applying_UML_and_Patterns)
  The standard text for use-case driven analysis and design; source of the
  boundary/control/entity style the guide follows. Use for: use-case
  realizations, GRASP responsibility assignment, VOPC-style class diagrams.
- [IBM Rational Unified Process — use-case realization and VOPC concept](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/engineering-lifecycle-management-suite)
  "VOPC" is RUP vocabulary. Use for: the formal definition of a View of
  Participating Classes and use-case realization.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/SoftwareEngineering](https://www.reddit.com/r/SoftwareEngineering/)
  Use for: sanity checks on design decisions and pattern roles.
- Course channel / classmates study group
  The highest-value community for an exam: past papers and the professor's
  marking habits. Use for: which alternative flows the professor likes to test.

- CS425 Sample Practice Examination (official; local file
  `~/Downloads/CS425_Practice_Question.pdf`, kept out of the public repo)
  The authoritative Part A format: 45 min, 30 marks — use-case diagram (8),
  design-level sequence diagram (12), VOPC (10). Use for: question style, mark
  weights, and the design-level participant list. Worked through in lesson 0009.
