# Mission: CS425 Part A — draw UML diagrams on paper

## Why
Pass Part A of the CS425 final exam. Part A is a 30–45 minute paper section
on analysis, UML diagrams, and design decisions. The skill is to draw correct
diagrams by hand, fast, from a written scenario.

## Success looks like
- Draw a complete use-case diagram (actors, boundary, «include», «extend») from a scenario in under 5 minutes.
- Write a full use-case specification with main, alternative, and exceptional flows.
- Draw a sequence diagram with boundary/control/entity/DAO participants in the correct order.
- Convert a sequence diagram to a collaboration diagram with correct message numbers.
- Draw a VOPC that is consistent with the interaction diagrams.
- Explain subsystem responsibilities, interfaces, and the façade/service/DAO/repository roles.
- Pass the professor's consistency check: the same scenario tells the same story in every artifact.

## Constraints
- Exam is paper-based. Practice must end on paper, not in a tool.
- Time pressure: the whole of Part A is 30–45 minutes.
- Study the six topics in the professor's order; each builds on the last.

## Out of scope
- Part B (laptop implementation, validation, persistence, testing).
- UML diagram types not in the guide (state, activity, deployment, component).
- CASE tools and diagram software.
